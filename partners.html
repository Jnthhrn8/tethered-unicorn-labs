03 / THE EXCHANGE

Useful support. Honest recognition. No glittery fine print.

Transparent credit

Approved partners may receive public acknowledgment, links, and an accurate description of what they contributed.

Measured field insight

Partners may receive carefully scoped, aggregated product feedback or case-study findings—never private user data.

Authentic recommendations

Affiliate links may support the lab, but only for relevant products and always with a clear disclosure near the recommendation.

Independent judgment

Support never purchases praise, ranking, exclusivity, favorable testing, or influence over privacy and safety decisions.
