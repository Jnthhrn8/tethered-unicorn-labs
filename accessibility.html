Accessibility

Useful technology should be easier to reach.

Updated October 5, 2026. We are improving the accessibility of the Tethered Unicorn Labs public website as it develops.

Using the site

Pages include headings, descriptive links, a skip-to-content link, visible keyboard focus, and layouts that adapt to smaller screens. Navigation and contact buttons can be used with a keyboard. Decorative animation is disabled when your device requests reduced motion.

Work in progress

We have not completed an independent accessibility audit and do not claim full conformance to an accessibility standard. Assistive-technology testing, contrast checks, and usability improvements are ongoing. Linked external sites and the separate Forge application may behave differently.

Report a barrier

Email tetheredunicorn@gmail.com with the page address, the task you were trying to complete, and the browser or assistive technology involved if you are comfortable sharing it. You can also contact us by phone or text. We will work with you on an alternative way to get the information.
