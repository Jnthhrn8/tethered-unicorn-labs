01 / THE LAB

AI should belong in your life—not own it.

We believe the next generation of personal computing will be adaptive, model-flexible, and capable of meaningful action. It will live across the user’s devices, preserve their agency, and show its work.

Our goal is not another isolated chatbot. We are creating an accountable workspace where people, models, tools, and trusted computers can work together.

03 / FLAGSHIP HARDWARE

FOUNDERS PROTOTYPE

UniCrypt

A self-contained compression and encryption appliance designed to keep the master key off the computer and operate without cloud services or installed desktop software.

  • Local-only browser interface over the device’s private Wi-Fi
  • Streaming AES-256-GCM encryption and DEFLATE compression
  • microSD storage with a separate physical hardware key

04 / OUR PRINCIPLES

Private by design

Local capability and deliberate data boundaries should be defaults, not premium upgrades.

Evidence over theater

An agent should verify what happened and admit what did not—not simply announce success.

Choice over lock-in

Models and providers will change. The user’s workspace, history, and agency should endure.

Human-governed autonomy

Powerful automation needs visible activity, scoped permissions, interruption, and recovery.

05 / COMPANY

Built for a more capable—and more human—future.

Tethered Unicorn Labs is an early-stage research and product company exploring personal AI, reliable agents, private compute, adaptive interfaces, and practical human–AI collaboration.

We are beginning with Unicorn Forge and a carefully controlled field-testing program. The mission is ambitious; the method is grounded in measurable usefulness, responsible experimentation, and continuous verification.
