THE PROBLEM

AI is impressive in a chat box—and fragmented everywhere else.

People juggle separate assistants, model subscriptions, automation tools, credentials, files, and devices. Most systems lose context, cannot reliably operate the computer, or claim success without proof.

Research creates another kind of fragmentation: scattered links, repeated searches, incomplete context, and conclusions that are difficult to check. A managed application can bring the workflow together and reduce setup and maintenance for customers.

SOFTWARE CAPABILITIES

From a question to evidence. From a task to an outcome.

01

AI workspace

Forge brings model selection, project files, conversation context, and tools together so users can work across a task without repeatedly starting over.

02

Agentic execution

Break a prompt into tasks, select appropriate models, call tools, show live commands and responses, and verify completion.

03

Personal capability layer

Connect skills, MCP servers, calendars, Obsidian, development tools, browser control, and user-managed credentials.

04

Conversational research

Irideselle Search starts with available clues, uses follow-up questions to refine the inquiry, and helps users review public-source findings.

05

Evidence and reporting

Irideselle Search captures source material, timestamps, and references, then organizes approved findings into case folders and reports for human review.

06

Observable autonomy

Expose agent activity, failures, reasoning artifacts, health, resource use, approvals, and human takeover instead of hiding them.

BUSINESS MODEL HYPOTHESES

Recurring revenue from useful software and dependable service.

  • Hosted subscriptions for Unicorn Forge and Irideselle Search, separately or as a suite
  • Usage-based tiers for research and agent workloads, subject to measured delivery costs
  • Team plans, onboarding, support, and workflow configuration
  • Optional deployment and integration services for organizations with specific requirements

These are hypotheses to validate during alpha; paid plans are not yet generally available. Pricing, retention, model and hosting costs, support demand, and gross margins must be measured before scaling.
